The family

Twenty-six years of the same chicken.

Noi started grilling gai yang at the Thai temple fair in Bonnyrigg in 1998. People queued for it every year, and eventually her son Kit gave the recipes a permanent home.

Isaan food is northeastern Thai cooking, close to what's eaten across the river in Laos. It's sour, smoky and hot, and it's eaten with your hands: tear off a piece of sticky rice, press it into the laab, repeat.

We make the sausage on Mondays when we're closed, pound every papaya salad to order, and only cook as hot as you ask. If it's your first time, tell us. We'll steer you.
