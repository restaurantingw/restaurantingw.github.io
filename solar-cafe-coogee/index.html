Do Brasil

The best goods from Brazil, on Dudley Street.

We're a little bit Brazil and a little bit Coogee. The cheese bread, the griddled bread and the coffees are from Brazil. The eggs, the avo and the big breakfasts are Sydney mornings, done the Solar way.

  1. Pão de queijo cheese bread

    Brazil's favourite snack. Small, warm, golden cheese bread to have with your coffee or take for the walk.

  2. Pão na chapa griddled bread

    Bread buttered and pressed on the griddle until it's crisp. A Brazilian breakfast in two bites.

  3. Unique coffees

    Pistachio latte, orange latte, churros coffee. And specialty coffee, done straight, for the purists.
