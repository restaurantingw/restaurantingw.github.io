The affair

Two cafes, one Indonesian heart.

We opened on Boundary Street in Darlinghurst in 2020, behind St Vincent's Hospital. In 2024 we opened a second cafe in Glebe, across the road from Foley Park.

The food is Indonesian at heart and Sydney brunch in shape: se'i beef in a potato bun, Javanese smashed chicken in focaccia, soto soup on the side for dipping. Coffee comes from Sample, focaccia from PiOik, and at Darlo the pastries come from Sonoma.

Come in, say hello, ask what's on special. That's the whole idea.
