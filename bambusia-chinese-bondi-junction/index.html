The family table

Four decades of sizzling plates on Newland Street.

Cantonese and Malaysian cooking, white tablecloths, and the same family at the door since around 1980.

Book a table · (02) 9387 5838 Directions

Bambusia is a family business. Richard Leong and his wife have run the dining room on Newland Street for the better part of forty years.

The menu is the one Bondi Junction grew up on: Cantonese classics with a Malaysian streak, cooked to order and brought to the table hot. The sizzling Mongolian lamb still comes out on the hot plate.

“Bambusia Bondi Junction has no other branches, is not associated with any other restaurant of the same name, and has no karaoke.”From our Facebook page

Some couples met over these tables. One of them, who first sat down here in 1983, told SBS their story in 2023.

Book by phone, come in a group, order too much. That's how it's done here.
