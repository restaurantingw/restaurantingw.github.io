Who we are

One kitchen, one cook, one plate at a time.

Akarui is the two of us: a couple, a small room with a few tables and one person cooking. Every order is made from the start when you place it.

The menu is mostly Japanese: sushi and sashimi, katsu, teriyaki, donburi with miso soup. Alongside it we cook a few Korean home-style dishes, like bulgogi soup in a clay pot, bibimbap and kimchi.

Because nothing is made ahead, a busy lunch can mean a short wait. We think it's worth it. If you're taking away, give us a call first and it'll be ready when you get here.

明るい (akarui) means bright, cheerful. That's how we'd like you to leave.
