Why taguán

a little place to feel at home.

Sydney, AU 2008. Baguio, PH 2600. Two postcodes, one café.

Hey dad,

We're two people who came to a new country and went looking for a sense of home. We found it here, on Abercrombie Street, so we opened a café and dedicated it to you.

Your adobo is on the menu. Rice used to be lunch-only, but people kept asking, so now Tatay's Adobo is on from sunrise. Banana ketchup goes in the brekkie burger. Adobo sauce goes in the Croque Mamser. Pandesal comes out of the oven when the season's right.

Everyone's welcome here. Locals, students, runners, dogs. We hope it feels a bit like home to them too.
