Two years on Riley St

Nothing sits around here.

The focaccia, the pies, the muffins and the biscuits come out of our oven every morning. The pasta is rolled here. The arancini are rolled here too.

Breakfast from 6am, a proper Italian lunch, coffee all day and a spritz when the week calls for it. Sit inside the ADGE or grab it from the window.

Gluten-free. Nut-free. Lactose-free. Flavour-free.We'll skip the last one.
