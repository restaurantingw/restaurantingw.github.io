Marrickville Rd · since 1980

One family, one rotisserie, forty-six years.

A Greek taverna on Marrickville Rd. Big plates, open kitchen, open late.

The spit is turning.

The Giannakelos family opened Corinthian in 1980, and it is still a family affair: Foti at the open kitchen, Maria in the back, and their sons George and Chris out front. Marrickville has changed around it. The servings haven't got any smaller.
