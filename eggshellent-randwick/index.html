Off the menu

More than eggs and coffee.

Overly friendly isn't a slogan, it's how we run the place. A few things you'll only find here.

  1. Thursday hills run

    Every Thursday we run the hills together. Finish the workout and your post-run coffee is on us.

  2. The pull-up bar

    Yes, there's a pull-up bar in the cafe. Our barista will challenge you. Better safe than sorry: order first.

  3. Birthday giveaway

    Every year on our birthday we throw a giveaway for the people who keep us going. Follow @eggshellentrandwick so you don't miss it.

  4. Prams and kids welcome

    Family friendly and pram friendly. Wheel straight in, we'll find you a spot.
