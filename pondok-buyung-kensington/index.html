Minang home cooking on Anzac Parade, the way it's eaten in Padang: rice, and whatever looks best in the window today.

There's no printed menu at Pondok Buyung. You walk up to the bain-marie, look at the curries and fried things laid out behind the glass, and point. That's nasi campur, and it's how Padang food has always worked.

The rendang is the one everyone asks for: beef cooked down slowly in coconut and spice until it's dark and dry. Goat curry, bakwan, cassava leaf curry and a sharp green sambal ijo sit alongside it.

Coconut, chilli, curries over rice.

We've been on Anzac Parade, almost opposite Peters of Kensington, for years. About forty seats, everything halal, and please bring cash.
