Good to know

Isn't Instagram enough?

Instagram is great for showing food. But people searching on Google want your hours, menu and a way to book or order. A website answers those in seconds, and shows up when people search your name or "Thai near me".

How is this different from Wix or Squarespace?

Those are tools; you still have to design, write, photograph and update the site yourself. We do all of it, design it around your place, and keep it current. You just text us when something changes.

Why is it so much cheaper than an agency?

We only build for restaurants and cafés, so we've streamlined everything from menus to opening hours to Google setup. You get custom design without paying for weeks of meetings.

Who owns the website and the domain?

You own your domain and your content. If you ever leave, we hand everything over.

What about photos?

We use your own photos, picked and edited to look their best. If you don't have good ones yet, we'll use tasteful placeholders until you do.

How do I make changes?

Send a text or an email with what's changed. We update it within 48 hours.

Do you work with cafés, bakeries and takeaways?

Yes. Any independent food business in Sydney: restaurants, cafés, bakeries, bars and takeaways.
