Crown St · Surry Hills

A North African family kitchen, on Crown St.

Moroccan, Algerian and Tunisian cooking from Mama Linda's kitchen. Halal.

Lunch, dinner and mint tea, Wednesday to Sunday.

Sanah and Nadim opened Café Tanja to cook the food they grew up with, and Sanah's mum, Mama Linda, runs the kitchen. The dishes are the ones you rarely find in Sydney: tchouktchouka, brik, msemen, and a tagine served with matlouh bread to mop it up.

Pull up a couch, pour the mint tea, stay a while.

The room is bright: painted walls, tiles, kilim rugs and couches Nadim built himself. Come for lunch, come back for dinner, and finish with a Zaazaa smoothie or a pot of Moroccan mint tea poured from up high.
